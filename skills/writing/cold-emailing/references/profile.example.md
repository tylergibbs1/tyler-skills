# Profile (example)

Copy this file to `profile.md` in the same folder and replace every line with your own. `profile.md` is gitignored, so a real employer and real numbers stay on your machine. Never commit it.

Everything below is invented.

## Who I am, in one sentence

I'm a backend engineer at a freight company.

## Proof lines

One per email. Each is something a stranger can picture or check, written the way you would say it.

| Proof | Line | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Dispatch rewrite | "Last year I rewrote our dispatch service and cut route planning from 40 seconds to 3." | Fleet, logistics, and infrastructure teams |
| Side project | "On my own time I built a tool that turns after-hours voicemails into booked appointments. Three dental offices use it." | Product and applied AI teams |

Wording that matters:

- The side project is a product with its own name. In a job email, describe what it does and leave the name out, so it reads as proof and not a pitch.
- Say "entered" for a competition you entered. Not "won," not "selected."

## Voice

Habits to copy, taken from emails you have sent:

- Greeting: "Hey {first name},"
- Subject: lowercase, "topic at company"
- Pet phrases, and how often: "Happy to be wrong here." once per email at most
- Sign-off: first name only

## Voice samples

Paste two or three emails you sent and liked, subject line included.

```
Subject: dispatch at acme

Hey Sam,

Saw your team is rebuilding dispatch for the new warehouses. Guessing the hard part is the last mile.

I'm a backend engineer at a freight company. Last year I rewrote our dispatch service and cut route planning from 40 seconds to 3.

I applied for the platform role. I want to work on this with your team.

You the right person to talk to?

Riley
```

## Cadence

Follow up five days after the first email. Two emails per reader, then stop.

## Never say

- Client names from the day job.
- Any number that is not in the proof lines above.
- Anything about pay or notice period. Ask me first.

## Links and handles

Site, GitHub, and social handles. These go in a reply when a reader asks for more, and I type links myself.
