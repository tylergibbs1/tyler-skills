## Evolution timeline

- **2022** — foundations: strategy serves business objectives; the tactics formula; fandom and human voice; one-message media discipline.
- **2023** — brand and value-prop discipline; crisis preparedness and apologies; audience-specific direct channels; launches, memeability, social proof.
- **2024** — the Go Direct manifesto: founder owns narrative and audience; Rostra launches (first project: Cognition's Devin).
- **2025** — don't outsource story or strategy; mission as recruiting and alignment engine; hook → story → channel; many-to-many media and employee voices.
- **2026** — direct content commoditized; narrative alpha moves to real work, evidence, craftsmanship, events, relationships; long-term trust over content volume; ship-to-yap ratio.

The deepest change: **communications itself cannot remain the differentiation once everyone has the playbook.** The advantage moves upstream into what the company actually does; competitors can't reproduce reality by copying the copy.

## Case studies she draws on

- **Anduril** — 10 rules; persuadable-middle targeting; mission-as-filter. Palmer Luckey: "Lulu was instrumental in putting Anduril on the map."
- **Substack** — VP Comms; concentric-circle rollouts to power users first; "My comment is www.substack.com/jobs"; Chris Best on Joe Rogan (chosen for format fit).
- **Activision Blizzard** — EVP Corporate Affairs/CCO from Oct 2022; "wartime" comms through Microsoft's $68.7B acquisition (closed Oct 13, 2023); company Substack, transparency report, public pushback on regulators; departed Jan 2024.
- **Rostra clients** — Cognition (Devin launch), Anduril, Coinbase, Ramp ("the Special Ops team of comms"), Suno, Midjourney. $40M venture fund disclosed Dec 2025.
- **Positive exemplars** — Tobi Lütke (early "going direct"), Brian Armstrong, Palmer Luckey.
- **Negative dissections** — TikTok's scripted CEO video ("trust is won or lost through body language — not words"), SBF's Vox interview, Southwest's 2022 non-apology.

## Sourcing caveats

- Much of her most detailed advice lives in podcasts, X threads, and Flack rather than formal publications; some widely circulated quotes come from third-party summaries and podcast note aggregators, which paraphrase. Prefer the primary Flack posts above when quoting.
- The verbatim headline wording of the Anduril 10 rules is corroborated via podcast transcript and chapter titles, not the paywalled essay; treat one-line rule names as near-verbatim.
- Her stances are deliberately combative and calibrated to founder-led, mission-driven tech companies; she says the approach doesn't work for "a professional CEO who is not actually interested in doing this themselves."
- Confidence is high on PR, messaging, copywriting, and mission (extensive primary sources); moderate on formal visual branding (her corpus covers positioning, symbols, and community far more deeply than design-system mechanics).
