# Writing

Copy skills that keep product writing accurate and human-sounding. All model-invoked.

- **[human-sounding-copy](./human-sounding-copy/SKILL.md)**: Make writing read as a specific human wrote it. Cuts AI verbosity, strips the lexical, punctuation, and formatting tells, then rebuilds with cohesion, rhythm, concreteness, stakes, and voice. Reference files carry the tell catalog, a list of exemplar writers to study before drafting, the craft canon, the idiolect method, and the research base.
- **[lulu-comms](./lulu-comms/SKILL.md)**: Apply Lulu Cheng Meservey's comms operating system to copy, mission, branding, and PR. Every tactic traces to a business objective through the belief → audience → action chain; hook before story before channel; evidence over adjectives. Reference files carry the copywriting templates, mission scorecard, positioning and fandom playbook, PR/crisis/apology playbooks, and the primary sources.
- **[cold-emailing](./cold-emailing/SKILL.md)**: Cold emails and DMs a stranger will read and answer. Five moves (them, a guess, one proof, pressure off, one question) in under 100 words, plus the practices behind them: who to write to, what counts as proof, how the email should read, sending only on the sender's say-so, and following up once. Ends with a before-and-after rewrite, the limits, and the sources.
